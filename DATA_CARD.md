# Data Card

## Intended use

This repository is for reviewing investigation practice, evidence discipline, calibration, and evaluation methodology. The worked case and calibration exercise are synthetic training artifacts.

## Contents and provenance

- Three case-study documents describing proposed audits
- A synthetic investigation scenario and invented dialogue
- A synthetic reviewer-calibration exercise
- Templates, workflow guidance, and metric definitions

No real user report, production transcript, model run dataset, or measured investigation-metrics dataset is published here. The synthetic dialogue was authored as an example and is not attributed to a model or provider.

## Collection and transformations

No production data was collected for the synthetic examples. Any future real case data must identify source, collection date, model/build where relevant, sampling configuration, transformations, and authorization status. Unknown provenance must remain marked unknown.

## Labels and review

Examples demonstrate provisional reasoning only. They are not an organizational taxonomy or adjudicated policy decision. Real labels require an applicable written policy and documented review.

## Limitations

- Synthetic examples do not establish model behavior or incident frequency.
- A small or curated set cannot represent a real case queue.
- A transcript may be incomplete and cannot alone prove tool use or root cause.
- Proposed metrics require definitions, appropriate sampling, and privacy review before use.

## Privacy and safety

Do not add real credentials, personal records, confidential medical data, or sensitive operational details. Keep originals in authorized systems; publish only reviewed and appropriately sanitized artifacts.
